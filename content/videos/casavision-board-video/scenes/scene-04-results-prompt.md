# Scene 04 — Results (42–55s)
## Atmospheric Background for Metric Card Overlays

---

## How to Use

1. Go to [runwayml.com](https://runwayml.com) and sign in.
2. Select **Gen-3 Alpha** → **Text to Video**.
3. Paste the prompt below into the text input field.
4. Apply the settings listed under **Runway Settings**.
5. Click **Generate** and produce **2–3 variations**.
6. Review each variation against the **Selection Criteria** below.
7. Export the best result and save as the filename specified.

> **Note:** This clip is a pure background layer. Metric card overlays (subscriber counts, revenue figures, growth percentages, etc.) will be layered on top of this background during the assembly step in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve. The background must be dark and calm enough to keep white text readable at all times.

---

## Runway Prompt — Paste This Exactly

```
Abstract dark blue and black background with subtle animated light particles and thin glowing lines slowly moving. Elegant, corporate, premium tech aesthetic. No text, no faces, no objects. Pure atmospheric background motion. 16:9.
```

---

## Runway Settings

| Setting       | Value        |
|---------------|--------------|
| Duration      | 13s          |
| Aspect Ratio  | 16:9         |
| Motion        | Very Slow    |

---

## Selection Criteria

Choose the variation that best meets **all** of the following:

- **Subtle, non-distracting motion** — particles and lines drift slowly; no sudden flashes, pulses, or rapid movement
- **Consistently dark** — the overall luminance stays dark throughout the full 13 seconds; no bright flare-ups or light areas that would make white text unreadable
- **No faces, people, text, logos, or recognizable objects** — this is a pure abstract background; reject any clip where a recognizable shape or face appears
- **Cool blue-black tones maintained** — dark navy and black should dominate; avoid warm amber, green, or red tints
- **Loopable feel (preferred)** — motion that looks like it could loop smoothly is ideal for the assembly editor

**Reject if:** any portion of the clip brightens significantly, a face or object is visible, text appears, or the motion is fast/chaotic rather than atmospheric.

## Alternative Prompt (If Results Are Unsatisfactory)

```
Dark navy blue gradient background with soft, slowly drifting bokeh light orbs and faint luminous particles. Deep, atmospheric, premium corporate aesthetic. No text, no faces, no recognizable shapes or objects. Pure abstract motion. Very slow movement throughout. 16:9.
```

**What changed:** "glowing lines" replaced with "bokeh light orbs" for a softer, less geometric feel; "gradient" added to ensure tonal depth across the frame; "faint luminous particles" keeps the motion subtle and avoids hard lines.
