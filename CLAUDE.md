# AI-Claude: NeoInsent HQ

Esteban's working repo for NeoInsent AI's non-code work: client proposals, product notes, marketing campaigns, decks, video content and Claude skills. App code does not live here.

## Layout
- `clients/<name>/` holds proposals, specs and generators per client (e.g. `clients/kimo`)
- `products/<name>/` holds product descriptions and specs (EV Karma, LeanWMS)
- `marketing/campaigns/` holds LinkedIn, Facebook and EU AI Act campaign drafts and logs
- `proposals/` holds pitch decks
- `content/videos/` holds video projects (HyperFrames)
- `agent/skills`, `.claude/`, `.agents/` hold installed Claude/agent skills (untracked)

## Code lives in ~/Developer
- `~/Developer/products/foottrack` (FootTrack app)
- `~/Developer/neoinsent/invoicing` (invoicing app; its data is in `~/NeoInsent Invoicing`)
- `~/Developer/community/techmaastricht` (TechMaastricht site)
- `~/Developer/tools/iphone-viewer`
- `~/Developer/external/` holds third-party clones (CleanFrame, career-ops)

## Rules
- New app or prototype: create it in `~/Developer`, not here.
