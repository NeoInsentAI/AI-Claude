## Intent

A short vertical piece for personal use — no brand framing, no CTA. It follows a
young adult through the disorienting experience of learning AI for the first
time: browser tabs and chat windows crowding the frame, claustrophobic and
cluttered, until the exact moment something clicks — the frame itself opens up
into one clean wide shot. Casual, relatable, feed-native tone, not a lecture.

## Assets

(none — fully invented visuals, no user-supplied material)

## Customizations

- Beat-synced reveal: the frame-opening moment is timed to land on a music beat.
- Fully invented visuals: recreated browser tabs, chat windows, and notes-app
  UI carry the piece. No real footage or photos.
- Design spec undecided: show 2-3 shipped presets to pick by eye during the
  storyboard pass rather than deciding now (deferred ask).

## Notes

- Personal/passion project — no consultant/brand voice, no call to action.
- Vertical aspect reinforces the "crowded" feeling early in the piece; the
  release into open space should land as a bigger relief than widescreen would
  give it.
