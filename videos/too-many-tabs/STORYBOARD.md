## Frame 1 — Too Many Tabs

- status: outline
- duration: 11s
- transition_in: cut
- scene: Recreated browser tabs, a chat window, and a notes app multiply and crowd inward around a blinking cursor, tightening the frame
- src: compositions/frames/01-struggle.html
- blueprint: overwhelm-surround (Problem role, hyperframes-animation) — adapted: no avatar-morph (no character is depicted; everything invented is UI), holds at peak crowding instead of resolving to a question card

Concept: the viewer is inside the exact feeling of being in over your head — not told about it, immersed in it. Every few seconds another surface opens: a new browser tab, a chat window shouldering in, a notes app wedging into a shrinking gap. The vertical canvas is already narrow; by the end of this beat it feels genuinely too small for what's being asked of it.

Mood: claustrophobic but not chaotic — controlled tightening, like a countdown, not noise. Late-night blue-white screen glow, warm room light barely visible at the very edges.

Choreography: each new surface SLAMS or WEDGES into frame from a different edge (top tab bar grows, chat bubbles PUSH up from the bottom, a notes panel SLIDES in from the side) — never the same entrance twice. A cursor blinks at dead center the whole time, the one fixed point everything else crowds toward. Cadence accelerates: slow-slow-faster-faster as more surfaces stack.

Depth layers: BG — dark UI chrome, faint desktop wallpaper barely visible between windows. MG — the stacking browser/chat/notes surfaces themselves. FG — the blinking cursor, always on top, always visible even as everything closes in around it.

SFX: notification pings and keyboard clicks layer in and multiply with each new surface, density and volume building — then a sudden hush in the last half-second before the cut, everything going quiet at once.

## Frame 2 — It Clicks

- status: outline
- duration: 9s
- transition_in: cut
- scene: Tight on the exact breakthrough detail — the cursor finally lands on a completed line — then one continuous decelerating zoom-out reveals a clean, spacious version of the same workspace, everything else closed
- src: compositions/frames/02-release.html
- blueprint: zoom-out-workspace-reveal (Hook/Benefits role, hyperframes-animation) — adapted: the "containing whole" it reveals is not a product workspace but the same desk, decluttered to one calm window

Concept: the payoff isn't triumphant, it's relief — the exhale after holding your breath. One small, specific thing finally works, and the frame itself rewards that by giving the viewer room to breathe for the first time in the video.

Mood: quiet confidence. Warm light now actually visible and filling the frame, not just at the edges. Unhurried — this beat trusts the stillness.

Choreography: open on an extreme-close macro of the cursor completing one small action (a line finishing, a checkmark landing) — held just long enough to register. Then ONE unbroken decelerating zoom-out pulls back to reveal everything else has cleared: one window, plenty of empty space, the room's warm light now visible in full. No new elements enter during the pull-back — the reveal is subtraction, not addition.

Depth layers: BG — warm, calm desk/room tone, now fully visible. MG — the single remaining clean window. FG — none needed; the emptiness is the point.

SFX: near-silence through the zoom-out, then one clean, warm chime exactly at the moment the pull-back locks into its final wide frame — timed to a music downbeat, not a fixed timecode.

## Notes

- Duration driver: the cut from Frame 1 to Frame 2, and the chime/lock moment inside Frame 2, are pinned to the music's beat grid once a track is chosen — not hand-set guesses. Real beat-grid timing overrides the estimated durations above.
- No spoken narration proposed — this reads as a near-wordless piece (visual + SFX + music carry it), which also works for feed viewers watching muted. Open to adding one short on-screen line at the very end if the silence reads as unclear rather than earned.
- Palette: dark, warm-tinted (not the cliché cyan/purple AI gradient) — an amber/warm-gold accent against near-black in Frame 1, opening into visible warm light in Frame 2. Design preset to be picked by eye from 2-3 shipped options at the sketch pass.
