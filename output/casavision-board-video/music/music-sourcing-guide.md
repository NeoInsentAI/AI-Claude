# Music Sourcing Guide — CasaVision Board Video

**Project:** 60-second AI-generated executive board video  
**Purpose:** Background music track — no lyrics, corporate/cinematic/inspirational  
**Save file as:** `background-track.mp3` in this folder (`output/casavision-board-video/music/`)

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Pixabay Music

1. Open your browser and go to: **https://pixabay.com/music/**
2. You will land on the Pixabay Music homepage, which shows featured tracks and a search bar at the top.
3. No account or login is required to preview tracks. You will need a free account only if prompted during download — registration is quick and free.

---

## Step 2: Search for Tracks

### Primary Search Terms (try these first)

In the search bar at the top of the page, type:

```
corporate cinematic inspire
```

Press Enter. Browse the results, listening for tracks that open softly and build toward a peak.

### Backup Search Terms (if primary results are not a good fit)

If the primary search does not surface suitable tracks, try each of the following one at a time:

| Search Term | When to Use |
|---|---|
| `motivational corporate` | If primary results feel too dramatic or too minimal |
| `epic corporate` | If you want a stronger, more powerful build |
| `inspiring business` | For a lighter, more upbeat corporate feel |
| `cinematic build` | If you want a pure cinematic arc with less corporate feel |

---

## Step 3: Selection Criteria — What to Listen For

Use this checklist as you preview tracks. A good track will check all five boxes:

- [ ] **Quiet opening (0–15 seconds):** The track starts gently — ambient pads, soft piano, or light strings. Not a loud intro.
- [ ] **Building mid-section (15–45 seconds):** Energy gradually increases. Additional instruments enter. You feel forward momentum.
- [ ] **Triumphant swell or peak (45–55 seconds):** The music reaches its emotional high point — full orchestra, swelling strings, or a strong melodic statement.
- [ ] **No lyrics:** The track must be fully instrumental. No vocals, no sung words at any point.
- [ ] **Duration 60–120 seconds:** Check the track length displayed next to the title. Anything in this range can be trimmed to exactly 60 seconds.

---

## Step 4: Preview Tracks on Pixabay

1. In the search results, each track shows a **play button (triangle icon)** on the left side of the row.
2. Click the play button to start streaming the track directly in your browser — no download required.
3. The playback bar at the bottom of the screen shows the current timestamp and total duration.
4. Use the timestamp bar to **skip to the middle** (around 30–40 seconds) to check the build, then **skip to 50 seconds** to hear the peak.
5. If a track sounds promising but you want to compare it to others, note the title and scroll down to keep previewing more results.

---

## Step 5: Download Your Selected Track

Once you have identified the right track:

1. Click on the **track title** to open its detail page (or look for the download icon in the results row).
2. On the detail page, click the **down-arrow / Download button** (it may say "Free Download" or show an arrow icon).
3. If prompted, log in or create a free Pixabay account.
4. Save the file with the name: **`background-track.mp3`**
5. Move or save the file to: `/Users/sera/AI-Claude/output/casavision-board-video/music/`

## Example Track Styles to Look For

These are **style descriptions**, not guaranteed real track names. Use them as a mental reference while previewing:

1. **"Corporate Motivation"** — steady 4/4 beat, light piano or guitar opening, full orchestra build at the midpoint, strong confident close. Feels like a product launch or leadership message.

2. **"Inspiring Cinematic"** — soft ambient pad intro, gradual string and brass layer-in, arrives at a warm triumphant swell around the 45-second mark. Cinematic but not dramatic.

3. **"Business Inspire"** — brighter tone, uplifting melody, moderate tempo. Feels optimistic and forward-moving throughout. Suitable for results and close segments of the video.
