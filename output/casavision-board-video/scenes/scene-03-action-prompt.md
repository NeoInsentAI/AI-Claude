## Services in Motion: Fiber, Canal Casa 36, and Partnerships

## Important: This Scene Uses 3 Separate Clips

Scene 3 is assembled from **3 separate Runway generations** (Vignettes A, B, and C). You do **not** generate this scene as a single clip.

**Workflow:**
1. Generate each vignette individually in Runway Gen-3 Alpha (instructions for each below).
2. Save each clip to the filename specified.
3. Bring all three clips into CapCut or DaVinci Resolve and cut them together following the Assembly Note at the bottom of this file.

Total assembled duration: **~20 seconds** (7s + 7s + 6s).

# Vignette A — Fiber Installation (7s)

## How to Use

1. Go to [runwayml.com](https://runwayml.com) and sign in.
2. Select **Gen-3 Alpha** → **Text to Video**.
3. Paste the prompt below into the text input field.
4. Apply the settings listed under **Runway Settings**.
5. Click **Generate** and produce **2–3 variations**.
6. Review each variation against the **Selection Criteria** below.
7. Export the best result and save as the filename specified.

---

## Runway Prompt — Paste This Exactly

```
A telecom technician in uniform installs fiber optic cable inside a bright modern home. A happy Nicaraguan family — mother, father, young child — stands in the background smiling. Warm indoor lighting. Cinematic, shallow depth of field. Corporate documentary style.
```

---

## Runway Settings

| Setting       | Value      |
|---------------|------------|
| Duration      | 7s         |
| Aspect Ratio  | 16:9       |
| Motion        | Slow       |

---

## Selection Criteria

Choose the variation that best meets **all** of the following:

- **Technician clearly working** — hand or tool visibly engaged with cable or wall panel, not just standing
- **Family visible and smiling** — soft background focus, clearly legible as a family unit (not a crowd or strangers)
- **Warm, residential feel** — indoor lighting should feel homey, not industrial or clinical
- **Shallow depth of field** — technician sharp in foreground, family slightly soft in background
- **No text or logos visible** — no signage, branded boxes, or on-screen titles

**Reject if:** family members look distressed or confused, setting looks like a server room, or image is dark/grainy.

---

## Output Filename

Save the selected clip as:

```
scene-03a-install.mp4
```

---

## Alternative Prompt (If Results Are Unsatisfactory)

```
A uniformed cable technician kneels near a wall panel inside a clean, well-lit family home, connecting fiber optic cables. In the background, a smiling family of three watches with satisfaction. Warm natural light from windows. Cinematic shallow depth of field. Professional, documentary-style cinematography.
```

**What changed:** "bright modern home" replaced with "clean, well-lit family home" for more grounded residential feel; "technician kneels near a wall panel" adds a more specific, believable action pose.

# Assembly Note — Cut Scene 3 Together

Once all three vignettes are generated and saved, assemble them in **CapCut** or **DaVinci Resolve**:

1. Import `scene-03a-install.mp4`, `scene-03b-studio.mp4`, and `scene-03c-corporate.mp4` into your timeline.
2. Place clips in order: **03a → 03b → 03c**.
3. Add a **0.3s dissolve transition** between 03a and 03b, and between 03b and 03c.
4. Verify total assembled duration is approximately **20 seconds** (7 + 7 + 6 = 20s, minus dissolve overlap ≈ 19.4s — this is acceptable).
5. Export the assembled sequence as `scene-03-action.mp4` for use in the master timeline.
