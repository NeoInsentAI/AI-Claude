# Scene 01 — Hook (0–8s)
## Cinematic Opening Over Managua at Dusk

---

## How to Use

1. Go to [runwayml.com](https://runwayml.com) and sign in.
2. Select **Gen-3 Alpha** → **Text to Video**.
3. Paste the prompt below into the text input field.
4. Apply the settings listed under **Runway Settings**.
5. Click **Generate** and produce **2–3 variations**.
6. Review each variation against the **Selection Criteria** below.
7. Export the best result and save as the filename specified.

---

## Runway Prompt — Paste This Exactly

```
Cinematic aerial drone shot slowly pulling back over Managua, Nicaragua at dusk. City lights begin to turn on across the urban landscape. Warm golden-hour light fading to deep blue. High altitude, wide establishing shot. Smooth camera movement. Photorealistic, 4K quality. No people in frame. Corporate documentary style.
```

---

## Runway Settings

| Setting       | Value      |
|---------------|------------|
| Duration      | 8s         |
| Aspect Ratio  | 16:9       |
| Motion        | Slow       |

---

## Selection Criteria

Choose the variation that best meets **all** of the following:

- **Smooth pull-back motion** — camera moves steadily away from the city with no stutter or jump cuts
- **Warm-to-blue dusk lighting** — golden-hour warmth visible transitioning toward deep twilight blue
- **City lights activating** — at least some visible light points appearing across the urban landscape
- **No jarring artifacts** — no flickering, distorted buildings, or visual glitches
- **No people in frame** — purely aerial, city-level view

---

Save the selected clip as:

scene-01-hook.mp4

## Alternative Prompt (If Results Are Unsatisfactory)

If the primary prompt does not produce satisfactory results, try this variation:

```
Cinematic aerial drone footage of a twilight cityscape, slowly zooming out over a Central American city. Urban lights gradually illuminate across the skyline. Smooth, slow zoom out. Golden warm tones transitioning to cool deep blue sky. Wide establishing shot from high altitude. No people. Photorealistic. Corporate documentary tone. 4K.
```

**What changed:** "dusk" replaced with "twilight cityscape," motion direction reframed as "slowly zooming out" for additional clarity, "Central American city" substituted to broaden model recognition while preserving geographic feel.
