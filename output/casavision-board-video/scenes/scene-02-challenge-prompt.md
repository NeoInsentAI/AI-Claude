# Scene 02 — Challenge (8–22s)
## Split-Screen: Market Competition vs. CasaVision Workers

---

## How to Use

1. Go to [runwayml.com](https://runwayml.com) and sign in.
2. Select **Gen-3 Alpha** → **Text to Video**.
3. Paste the prompt below into the text input field.
4. Apply the settings listed under **Runway Settings**.
5. Click **Generate** and produce **2–3 variations**.
6. Review each variation against the **Selection Criteria** below.
7. Export the best result and save as the filename specified.

---

## Runway Prompt — Paste This Exactly

```
Split-screen video. Left side: blurred, abstract urban telecom imagery, soft focus competitor billboard, indistinct. Right side: two telecom technicians in uniforms laying fiber optic cable in a residential neighborhood street, daytime, natural light, focused and professional. Clean visual divide down the center. Corporate documentary style. 16:9. Smooth motion.
```

---

## Runway Settings

| Setting       | Value      |
|---------------|------------|
| Duration      | 14s        |
| Aspect Ratio  | 16:9       |
| Motion        | Moderate   |

---

## Selection Criteria

Choose the variation that best meets **all** of the following:

- **Clear split-screen composition** — a clean, visible vertical divide separating both halves throughout the clip
- **Right side vivid and in focus** — workers and fiber cable activity appear sharp, well-lit, and professional
- **Left side muted and indistinct** — competitor imagery appears blurred, soft, and low-contrast
- **No unnatural distortions** — no body part deformations, melting edges, or visual artifacts on either side
- **Consistent motion** — both sides move naturally without snapping or looping incorrectly

---

## Output Filename

Save the selected clip as:

```
scene-02-challenge.mp4
```

---

## Fallback: If Split-Screen Does Not Render Correctly

Runway Gen-3 Alpha can struggle to maintain a persistent split-screen layout. If the composition breaks down across variations, **use two separate shots** instead:

1. **Shot A (Left side replacement):** Generate a standalone clip of blurred, abstract urban telecom imagery — soft-focus street signage, indistinct billboards, bokeh lights. Duration: 7s.
2. **Shot B (Right side replacement):** Generate a standalone clip of two telecom workers in uniform laying fiber optic cable on a residential street, daytime, natural light. Duration: 7s.

Cut between the two shots in your assembly timeline. Use a hard cut or a fast dissolve (3–5 frames) at the midpoint.

---

## Alternative Prompt (If Results Are Unsatisfactory)

If the split-screen prompt does not produce usable results, drop the split-screen entirely and use this single-shot version:

```
Two telecom technicians in company uniforms crouching on a residential street, carefully laying fiber optic cable into a conduit. Daytime. Natural sunlight. Background buildings and street softly blurred with shallow depth of field bokeh effect. Professional, focused expressions. Corporate documentary style. Smooth camera movement. 16:9. Photorealistic.
```

**What changed:** Split-screen removed entirely. Workers remain the primary subject. Competitor context is implied through the blurred background rather than a side-by-side composition.

## Text Overlay — Scene 02

### Overlay Text

> **"Un Mercado en Crecimiento. Expectativas Más Altas."**

### Instructions (Create in Canva)

1. Open [canva.com](https://canva.com) and create a **Custom Size** design: **1920 × 1080 px**.
2. Set the background to **transparent** (requires Canva Pro; alternatively use a black background and set opacity to 0% in your video editor).
3. Add a **Text** element and type:
   ```
   Un Mercado en Crecimiento. Expectativas Más Altas.
   ```
4. Font: **Montserrat SemiBold**
5. Size: **~55px** (adjust so text spans roughly 60–70% of canvas width)
6. Color: **White (#FFFFFF)**
7. Position: **Lower-center** — horizontally centered, approximately 80–85% down from the top of the canvas
8. Apply a **Drop Shadow** effect:
   - Color: Black (#000000)
   - Opacity: 60–70%
   - Blur: 4–6px
   - Offset X: 0, Offset Y: 2px
9. Export as **PNG with transparent background**.

### Output Filename

Save the overlay as:

```
overlays/scene-02-title.png
```
