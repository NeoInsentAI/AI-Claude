# CasaVision 2025 Annual Board Video — Production Brief Spec

## Overview

**Client:** Casavisión (casavision.com)
**Deliverable:** AI-generated executive video with narration
**Format:** 16:9, 1080p, ~60 seconds
**Purpose:** 2025 Annual Performance Review for internal Board of Directors
**Narrative approach:** The Year in Story (Challenge → Action → Result)
**Language:** Spanish (Latin American neutral accent)

---

## Tone & Style

- **Voice:** Confident, proud, grounded — executive tone, not sales
- **Music:** Ambient/cinematic open → builds through Action → triumphant swell at Results → clean fade on close
- **Narrator:** Single professional voice, measured pace (~140 wpm)
- **Aesthetic:** Premium corporate — dark backgrounds for metric scenes, naturalistic for action vignettes

## Scene-by-Scene Production Brief

---

### [SCENE 1] Hook — 0–8s

**Visual:**
Aerial drone shot over Managua at dusk, city lights coming on. Slow pull-back. CasaVision logo fades in center frame.

**On-screen text:**
`2025 Annual Review`

**Narration:**
> "En 2025, el panorama de conectividad de Nicaragua cambió — y Casavisión estuvo en el centro de ese cambio."
>
> *(In 2025, Nicaragua's connectivity landscape changed — and Casavisión was at the center of that change.)*

**Music:** Low ambient tone, single piano note.

---

### [SCENE 2] Challenge — 8–22s

**Visual:**
Split screen — one side shows abstract competitor imagery (blurred/generic), other side shows CasaVision fiber lines being laid in a neighborhood. Transitions to a stylized map of Managua highlighting unserved zones in red.

**On-screen text:**
`Un Mercado en Crecimiento. Expectativas Más Altas.`
*(A Growing Market. Rising Expectations.)*

**Narration:**
> "La demanda de fibra de alta velocidad alcanzó niveles récord. Los clientes exigían más: velocidades más rápidas, instalaciones más ágiles, y una empresa que realmente los conociera. La presión era real."
>
> *(Demand for high-speed fiber reached record highs. Customers expected more — faster speeds, faster installs, and a company that actually knew their name. The pressure was real.)*

**Music:** Slight tension build, low strings.

---

### [SCENE 3] Action — 22–42s

**Visual:**
Three fast-cut vignettes cycling in sequence:
1. Technicians installing fiber cable in a residential home — family smiling in background
2. Canal Casa 36 broadcast studio with presenter on air
3. Corporate client handshake in a CasaVision branch office

**On-screen text (cycling with each vignette):**
- `18 Nuevos Barrios`
- `680 Empleados Directos`
- `3,100 Cuentas Corporativas`

**Narration:**
> "Nos expandimos a 18 nuevos barrios en Managua y Ciudad Sandino. Crecimos hasta 680 empleados directos — sin subcontrataciones, sin atajos. Consolidamos nuestra cartera corporativa con más de 3,100 cuentas empresariales. Y mantuvimos Canal Casa 36 como el canal de la comunidad — llegando a 210,000 televidentes cada semana."
>
> *(We expanded into 18 new neighborhoods across Managua and Ciudad Sandino. We grew our team to 680 direct employees — no subcontractors, no shortcuts. We deepened our corporate portfolio to over 3,100 business accounts. And we kept Canal Casa 36 as the community's channel — reaching 210,000 viewers every week.)*

**Music:** Builds, confident rhythm, growing momentum.

---

### [SCENE 4] Results — 42–55s

**Visual:**
Dark background. Four large metric cards appear one by one with animated counter effect:
1. `52,800 Suscriptores Activos`
2. `+31% Crecimiento en Ingresos`
3. `99.8% Disponibilidad de Red`
4. `NPS 81`

**On-screen text:** Same as metric cards (animated counter on numbers).

**Narration:**
> "Los resultados hablan por sí solos. Más de 52,000 suscriptores activos. Ingresos con un crecimiento del 31%. Disponibilidad de red del 99.8%. Y un NPS de 81 — uno de los más altos en nuestra historia."
>
> *(The results speak for themselves. Over 52,000 active subscribers. Revenue up 31%. Network uptime at 99.8%. And a customer satisfaction score of 81 — among the highest in our history.)*

**Music:** Full triumphant swell — peaks at this scene.

---

### [SCENE 5] Close — 55–60s

**Visual:**
CasaVision logo centered on clean dark background. Tagline fades in below logo.

**On-screen text:**
`Con Casavisión, sentite en casa.`

**Narration:**
> "Casavisión. 100% Nicaragüense."

**Music:** Fades cleanly to silence, holds on logo.
