## POST 3 — HOW TO AUDIT YOUR PROCESSES FOR AI READINESS
**Scheduled:** 2026-06-19T20:00:00

### LinkedIn
Before you buy any AI tool — do this audit first.

Most companies skip this step and end up with expensive tools that nobody uses.

The 5-step AI readiness audit:

1. List your top 10 most time-consuming processes
2. Circle every task that is repetitive and rule-based
3. Rate each on data availability (do you have the data to feed an AI?)
4. Rank by time cost × frequency × error rate
5. Your #1 ranked item = your first AI target

This takes 2 hours. It can save you months of wasted AI implementation.

## POST 13 — AI SECURITY RISKS
**Scheduled:** 2026-06-29T20:00:00

### LinkedIn
AI creates value. But it also creates new risks — and most mid-size companies aren't prepared for them.

4 AI security risks you need to manage today:

**1. Data Leaks** — Sending sensitive data to public AI tools is a compliance risk. Fix: Use private deployments or enterprise-tier tools with data isolation.

**2. AI Bias** — AI models can produce discriminatory or inaccurate outputs. Fix: Audit outputs regularly, especially in HR and finance.

**3. Vendor Lock-In** — Over-relying on one AI vendor leaves you exposed if they change pricing or terms. Fix: Negotiate data portability before you sign.

**4. Shadow AI** — Employees using unauthorized AI tools without IT oversight. Fix: Create a company-wide AI usage policy now.

Security isn't a reason to avoid AI. It's a reason to implement it properly.

Has your company developed an AI security policy?
