Built by the community it serves

TechMaastricht started with a simple observation: this city sits at the meeting point of four countries, but its tech talent had no shared home. We're building one.

Our story

Maastricht has always punched above its weight — a historic university city, a magnet for international students, and a short drive from three other countries. What it lacked was a single place where developers, students, founders, and companies from across that region could actually find each other.

TechMaastricht exists to close that gap: one community, built locally, with a mission that was cross-border from day one.

Our mission

To grow a thriving tech talent base in Maastricht and South Limburg, and to make the city a genuine connection point for tech collaboration with Germany, Belgium, and Luxembourg.

Our vision

A region where a developer in Maastricht, a founder in Aachen, and a hiring manager in Liège are all part of the same community — not separated by a border that's a twenty-minute drive wide.
