Six ways to grow with the community

Every program exists to do one of two things: help you build tech skills, or help you find the people who can use them — across Maastricht and the wider border region.

The Six Pillars

01 — COMMUNITY

Meetups

Recurring local meetups and socials for developers and tech enthusiasts across South Limburg — lightning talks, demo nights, and simply a reason to get out from behind the screen and meet the people building alongside you.

02 — CAREER

Mentorship

We pair experienced tech professionals with newcomers and career-switchers, matched on goals rather than just job titles. Mentors commit to a few hours a month; mentees get a real sounding board for the decisions that matter most early in a career.

03 — SKILLS

Workforce Training

Part-time, bootcamp-style upskilling programs built around the roles regional employers are actually hiring for — not a generic curriculum, but one shaped by conversations with the companies doing the hiring.

04 — PIPELINE

Student & Youth Outreach

Partnership programs with Maastricht University, Zuyd Hogeschool, and local secondary schools — introducing students to tech careers early and giving them a direct line into the community before they graduate.

05 — ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Startup Support

Office hours, warm introductions, and a peer network for early-stage tech founders based in or building for the region — because a great idea shouldn't stall for lack of the right connection.

06 — REGIONAL

Cross-Border Exchange

Joint programs and events run together with tech communities in Aachen (Germany), Liège (Belgium), and Luxembourg City — shared hackathons, speaker exchanges, and a growing sense that this is one talent market, not four separate ones.
